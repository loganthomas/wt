Human output is colored by status on a terminal: `wt doctor`'s ok/warn/fail, tree and pool-slot states, and errors. `ui.color` (`auto`, `always`, `never`) controls it, and `NO_COLOR` is honored. Narrow terminals shorten long paths in the middle and wrap `wt doctor`'s text. `wt ls` and `wt status` show a header row, clean/dirty state, HEAD, age, and (with a `refresh_if_changed` gate) whether the refresh hook is fresh, with the path last and home shortened to `~`. In pool mode `wt ls` lists every slot with its lease and claim age, replacing `wt pool ls`. `--porcelain` is unchanged. (#23)
